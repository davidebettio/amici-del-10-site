Privacy policy

Amici del 10 · last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: Amici del 10 is designed for children and collects no personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services.

Controller

Davide Bettio — davide.bettio@gmail.com

Data stored on the device

To make the game work, the app stores only on the device:

  • the name, avatar and colour chosen in the profile (the name can be made up);
  • best scores and game statistics;
  • settings (sound, music, vibration, difficulty, language).

This data is never sent to us or to third parties. Deleting the app erases it.

Game Center

If Apple's Game Center is on, at the end of a game the score is sent to the Game Center leaderboard. The service is run by Apple under its own privacy policy; we do not receive any personal data about the player. For children's accounts, parents can turn Game Center off in Screen Time. The game works without it too.

Children

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from any other user. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no links to external websites.

Your rights

Since we do not process personal data, we hold no data to access, correct or delete. For any question, write to the address above.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page, with the date of the update.
