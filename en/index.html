Amici del 10

The game for practising the pairs of numbers that make 10.

Numbers move along a track: for each one, tap its “friend” on the keypad, the number that adds up to 10 with it. The longer you play, the faster the numbers arrive. The game ends when the first number reaches the end of the track.

Frequently asked questions

What is the leaderboard?
If Game Center is on, the Leaderboard button shows the Game Center leaderboard. Otherwise it shows “My records”, your 10 best scores saved only on the device.

How do I turn Game Center on or off?
In Settings › Game Center on the device. For children's accounts, parents can manage it in Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions. The game works without it too.

How do I reset records and stats?
They are stored only on the device: deleting the app erases them.

Which languages are available?
English and Italian. The app follows the device language; you can change it in the app's Settings.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No. No ads, no in-app purchases, no links to the outside.
